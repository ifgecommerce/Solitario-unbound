Support

Email info@ifgecommerce.com for help with Solitaire Unbound.

Include your device model, iOS version and app version. Explain what happened and how to reproduce it. Send screenshots only when helpful, with personal information hidden.

Never send passwords, passcodes, payment card details or Apple account credentials.
