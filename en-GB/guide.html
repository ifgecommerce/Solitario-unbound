Help and rules

Move all cards to the foundations or complete all eight Spider runs.

Build down in alternating red and black. Build each suit from Ace to King in the four foundations. Only a King may start an empty column.

All cards are visible. Use four free cells as temporary storage. Build down in alternating colors on the tableau and up by suit from Ace to King in the foundations.

Build same-suit runs from King to Ace. Place a card on the next higher rank; only same-suit runs move together. Fill empty columns before dealing.

Moving a run needs enough free cells and columns. The app only allows moves that can be performed one card at a time.

Hint

Help is free. Assisted results are labeled separately, with no paid penalties.

Daily challenge

The challenge depends on the date and rules version. No daily downloads are needed.

On-device data

Progress is local and does not sync between devices. Uninstalling the app may delete it.

Lifetime Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase for this app. It does not unlock other IFG apps.

The game works offline. Buying or restoring Pro may need a connection and an Apple account.

Privacy

No IFG account, ads or ad tracking. Games and statistics stay on your device.
